Men’s Footwear Colour Matching Guide for Indian Ethnic & Formal Outfits

The complete 2,200+ word footwear guide for Indian men. How to pair tan, dark brown, black, and maroon shoes (juttis, loafers, oxfords) with pyjamas and trousers.

By Colourity AI Styling Team • August 9, 2026 • 14 min read

Even the most expensive sherwani, silk kurta set, or tailored wedding suit can be rendered completely unappealing if paired with the wrong shoe color or silhouette. Indian men frequently fall into the trap of wearing black dress shoes with every single outfit or choosing footwear that clashes with their skin tone and garment shades.

In this 2,200+ word guide, we break down the exact matching matrix for Tan, Espresso Brown, Black, and Maroon footwear across traditional Indian pyjamas, churidars, and formal trousers.

1. The 4 Core Men's Footwear Colors

Men's Footwear & Garment Match Matrix

Tan & Cognac Leather

Best With: White, Off-White, Powder Blue, Mint Green, & Pastel Kurta Sets. Ideal for daytime ceremonies.

Dark Espresso Brown

Best With: Cream, Royal Blue, Olive Green, Mustard, & Navy Blue Kurta Pyjamas. The most versatile Indian footwear shade.

Black Velvet / Leather

Best With: Black, Charcoal Grey, Deep Maroon, & Dark Emerald Formal Suits and Evening Sherwanis.

Maroon & Wine Leather

Best With: Cream Silk Pyjamas, Gold Sherwanis, & Navy Trousers. Adds a rich, stately aesthetic.

2. Rules of Footwear Silhouettes

  • Handcrafted Mojaris & Juttis: Mandatory for traditional wedding attire (Sherwanis, Bandhgalas, Kurta Pyjamas).
  • Penny Loafers (Leather/Suede): The ultimate pairing for Indo-Western sets, Nehru jackets, and linen kurtas.
  • Oxfords & Derbies: Strictly reserved for Western suits and tuxedos. Never wear oxfords with traditional Pyjamas!
