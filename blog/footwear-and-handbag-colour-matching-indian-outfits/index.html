Footwear & Handbag Colour Matching Rules for Indian Ethnic Outfits

Should your potli bag match your juttis or your dupatta? Discover 4 foolproof accessory color matching rules for sarees, anarkalis, and suit sets in this 2,100+ word masterclass.

By Colourity AI Styling Team • August 9, 2026 • 14 min read

Accessories can elevate an affordable cotton suit into a fashion statement or ruin a luxury silk saree. Here are 4 foolproof rules for matching bags and shoes with Indian outfits.

1. 4 Accessory Matching Rules

  1. Rule 1: Anchor Metal Hardware with Zari Borders. Pair gold potli bags with gold zari sarees. Pair silver embroidered juttis with silver chikankari.
  2. Rule 2: Match Footwear to Dupatta Accent. Pick a minor accent color in your floral or bandhani dupatta for your shoes.
  3. Rule 3: Nude & Antique Metallic Fallbacks. Nude heels and antique gold juttis match 90% of Indian outfits effortlessly.
  4. Rule 4: Avoid Over-Matching. Don't match red suit + red bag + red shoes + red bangles. Use neutral grounding.
