Unstitched Suit Buying Guide: How to Pick Fabric Colours That Suit You Before Stitching

Save thousands in tailor fees with this 2,200+ word masterclass. How to test unstitched cotton, silk, and chanderi fabric colors against your skin undertone before making a purchase.

By Colourity AI Styling Team • August 9, 2026 • 15 min read

Shopping for unstitched dress materials in local bazaars is a beloved tradition in India. However, nothing is more disappointing than spending ₹2,000 on fabric and ₹1,500 on tailor stitching fees, only to put on the finished suit and discover the color makes your skin look tired or dull.

1. The Daylight Fabric Drape Test

  • Step 1: Step Away from Store Lights. Store fluorescent bulbs are cold and artificial. Always view unstitched fabric near natural daylight near a window or doorway.
  • Step 2: Hold Fabric Under Your Jawline. Observe your eye circles and complexion. If your face lights up, the color is in your palette. If your skin looks greyish, put it back.
  • Step 3: Use Scan Before You Buy on Colourity. Use Colourity's camera scanner while shopping in market stores to check palette fit before buying!
