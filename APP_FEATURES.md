# Colourity — Feature List

*Colour, Intelligent Technology For You.* A personal styling app powered by Colourity AI that tells you what
colours actually suit you, turns your existing clothes into a digital wardrobe, and puts together
outfits from what you already own — pointing you to a real product to buy only when nothing in
your wardrobe fills the gap.

---

## Personal Color Analysis

**Colour Scan.** The user takes a face scan; Colourity AI analyses skin undertone and determines
their seasonal colour palette (e.g. "Neutral Classic," "Deep Autumn"). Results are shown in
plain, human language — "warm and deep," "cool and vivid" — never the technical season name
itself, alongside a personalised set of colours generated toward real Indian fashion-retail
vocabulary (mustard, peacock blue, mehendi green, gulmohar orange) rather than generic swatches.

**First Match Reveal.** A dedicated celebration moment the first time the app produces a genuine
outfit match from the user's own colours and wardrobe — the app's first real "this works for you"
payoff, separate from account setup or onboarding.

**Colour Passport.** A shareable summary card of the user's colour profile and best palette —
built for showing off or saving, not just for internal use.

---

## Digital Wardrobe

**Garment Scanning.** Photograph any item of clothing and Colourity AI automatically extracts
category, colour, pattern, and formality — no manual tagging required. Supports scanning multiple
items in one session.

**Wardrobe Library.** Every scanned garment lives in a personal digital closet, viewable and
manageable at any time, each with its own detail view (photo, attributes, how it's been used in
outfits).

**Product-Photo Wardrobe Add.** Garments can also be added straight from a product photo (e.g. an
image shared in from Amazon/Myntra while shopping), with the same automatic attribute extraction.

---

## Outfit Matching

**Outfit Wizard.** Pick a base garment and an occasion; the app builds complete outfit
combinations from the user's own wardrobe using a fixed, deterministic matching engine — colour
harmony, pattern compatibility, and formality tiers computed in code from attributes Colourity AI
already extracted, not decided fresh by Colourity AI every time, so results stay consistent.

**Surprise Me.** A one-tap outfit suggestion, weather-aware (pulls current conditions to avoid
suggesting the wrong garment for the day) and occasion-aware, for when the user doesn't want to
build an outfit manually.

**Today's Pick / Today's Outfit.** A daily, ready-made outfit suggestion surfaced on Home, plus a
way to log what was actually worn that day.

**Explore Outfits & Styles.** A browsable feed of full outfit looks (own wardrobe or curated
inspiration), with tappable "hotspots" over each garment in a look — tap any item to see its
details and shop something similar.

**Layering: Outerwear & Footwear.** Dedicated flows for adding a jacket/coat or completing an
outfit with the right footwear, matched the same deterministic way as the rest of the wardrobe.

---

## Shopping Intelligence ("Scan Before You Buy")

**Scan Before You Buy.** Before purchasing a new item, the user can scan it (or share in a product
photo) and the app checks it against their existing wardrobe and colour profile — flags likely
duplicates, checks palette fit, and scores general versatility, so the purchase decision is
informed rather than impulsive.

**Shop the Gap.** When no existing wardrobe item completes a look, the app generates a live,
keyword-based search link to a real retailer (Myntra, Ajio, Amazon, Flipkart) for the *type and
colour* of item needed — never a fixed product link, always a fresh search built from the actual
recommendation.

**Shop Complete Look / Shop Similar.** From any outfit or garment hotspot, jump straight to a
retailer search for that specific piece, or open a platform picker to search the whole look across
all four marketplaces at once.

**Shopping History.** A record of past "Scan Before You Buy" checks and outcomes.

---

## Personalisation & Guidance

**Guided Product Tour.** A first-run, in-context walkthrough that teaches each major feature by
spotlighting the real element on the real Home screen — never a separate slideshow — and stays
permanently reachable afterward via a "Learn" entry point on each feature.

**Colourity Says.** A personalised insight line on Home, generated from the user's own stored
colour profile and wardrobe data (not freely generated by Colourity AI at display time), giving a specific,
data-backed nudge rather than a generic tip.

**Milestones & Achievements.** Progress tracking and a completion badge as a user builds out their
wardrobe and finishes their first full styling journey — shown permanently on their Profile once
earned.

**Stylist Summary.** A quick, always-visible summary on Profile of the user's current style
identity (colour season description + style archetype, in plain language).

---

## Account & Membership

**Google Sign-In.** Real Google authentication, no anonymous/guest accounts — offered
contextually the moment a real feature is used, not as a forced wall before the app can be
explored.

**Membership (Colourity Premium).** A subscription tier (with a real 7-day free trial via Google
Play Billing) unlocking unlimited styling and scanning, alongside a free tier with a limited scan
allowance.

**Buy Scans.** One-time, non-subscription scan credit packs for users who want more scans without
a full subscription.

**Profile Hub.** One place for account info, wardrobe/saved-look stats, scan balance, membership
status, and the stylist summary — with membership placed first as the primary path, scan balance
as the secondary utility.

**Saved Looks.** Outfits and looks the user has explicitly bookmarked, kept separate from the
auto-generated wardrobe/outfit history.

---

## Platform & Trust

**Dark Mode.** Full dark theme support across the app, not a partial/inconsistent implementation.

**Privacy Policy.** In-app, accessible privacy documentation.

**Weather-Aware Styling.** Local weather is factored into daily outfit and Surprise Me suggestions
so recommendations match real conditions, not just the calendar.

---

*This list reflects what's actually built in the codebase as of Aug 2026, not aspirational or
planned-only features.*
