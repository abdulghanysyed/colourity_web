You see a shirt, jacket, trouser, or pair of shoes that looks great on its own. You buy it. Then a few weeks later, it sits unworn because of these five common issues:

02

03

NOT VERSATILE

04

WARDROBE MISMATCH

05

REGRET

I like it now, but may not wear it later.
