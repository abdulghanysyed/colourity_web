The Problem

A Great Piece Isn't Always a Great Purchase

You see a shirt, jacket, trouser, or pair of shoes that looks great on its own. You buy it. Then, a few weeks later:

  • —It doesn't work with your existing clothes
  • —You have very few ways to wear it
  • —You already own something similar
  • —The colour doesn't integrate easily
  • —It only works for one occasion
  • —It becomes another isolated piece

A good purchase isn't only about liking the item. It's also about how well the item fits into the wardrobe you already have.

The Check

Evaluate a Garment Against Your Existing Closet

Before buying a new shirt or saree online or in-store, evaluate it with Colourity. The app checks whether the new item fits your colour profile and how it relates to pieces you already own — potential combinations, colour relationships, and whether it overlaps with something you already have.

The question this answers isn't "does this look good?" It's "can I actually use this?"

Versatility

How Many Outfits Can This New Piece Actually Unlock?

A garment that pairs with several pieces you already own gives you more useful combinations than one that only works with a single outfit. Colourity checks whether a potential purchase pairs with at least three pieces already in your wardrobe — a practical, concrete versatility check rather than a vague "more outfits is better" score.

When It's a Yes

Shop the Gap

Sometimes the result isn't "don't buy this." Sometimes it identifies a genuinely useful gap in your wardrobe.

Example

You have navy trousers, white shirts, and brown shoes — but no versatile jacket to connect them. Shop the Gap identifies the missing piece type and colour, and generates a live search link to a real retailer for that item.

Where verified, these search links point to Myntra, Ajio, Amazon, and Flipkart. Colourity isn't a marketplace — it generates a fresh search for the type and colour of item needed, rather than selling anything itself.
