## B. Links retained

135 blog → blog links were reviewed individually (source H1 vs. target H1, not just slug pattern-matching). 134 were judged genuinely relevant and retained as-is — the great majority connect posts that share occasion, garment type, or a direct "answers the next question a reader would ask" relationship (e.g. `whats-my-colour-season` ↔ `warm-vs-cool-vs-neutral-undertone` ↔ `how-do-i-know-what-colors-suit-me`, or `contrast-dupatta-matching-rules-plain-suits` ↔ `pair-contrasting-kurta-and-bottom-colors`). 76 blog → pillar contextual links and (after the Section A trim) 22 pillar → blog links were also reviewed; all retained links were judged to have a clear, specific reason a reader on that page would want that link, not just topical adjacency.

## C. Scan Before You Buy linking assessment

This is the one cluster that doesn't hold up to strict scrutiny, and the review treated it accordingly rather than papering over it.

**Site-wide, there is exactly one blog post that is genuinely about evaluating a purchase**: `/blog/unstitched-suit-fabric-buying-guide-skin-tone/` (picking fabric colour *before* buying/stitching a suit — a real pre-purchase decision). Every other post on the site was checked for buy/purchase/shopping/duplicate language, and while several mention shopping in passing, none are centrally about the act of deciding whether to buy something.

After Section A's removals, `/scan-before-you-buy/`'s "Related Masterclass Guides" section now contains **only that one link**. This is intentionally thin rather than padded — per the brief, relevance outranks link count. It does mean this pillar's supporting-content section is visibly sparser than the other three, which is a genuine content gap (see recommendation below), not a linking defect.

The blog → pillar contextual link into `/scan-before-you-buy/` was already conservative before this review (only `unstitched-suit-fabric-buying-guide-skin-tone` links to it in body copy) and needed no change.

## G. Final recommendation

**PASS.**

The internal-link graph is now relevance-first rather than count-first: 134 blog → blog links, 76 blog → pillar links, and 22 pillar → blog links, all individually reviewed and judged contextually genuine — down from the previous pass's 135/76/26, with every removal justified rather than arbitrary. Zero orphans, zero broken links, all SEO metadata (`<title>`, meta description, canonical, H1/H2/H3, schema, sitemap, robots.txt, images) confirmed unchanged.

Two follow-ups are worth planning as separate work, not part of this linking phase:
1. **Content, not linking**: `/scan-before-you-buy/` has only one strong supporting post. A follow-up content phase targeting purchase-evaluation, duplicate-avoidance, and wardrobe-compatibility queries would let this pillar's linking match the other three in depth.
2. **Editorial decision, not linking**: the 4-post foundational Colour Analysis cluster in Section D competes for near-identical search intent. Worth a deliberate call on whether to merge, differentiate, or leave as-is — this phase intentionally made no changes there.
